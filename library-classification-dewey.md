## Dewey-Adjacent Classification Notes

This system adapts Dewey Decimal Classification principles (hierarchical numerical organization,
subject-based grouping, cross-referencing) to a specialized culinary library focused on:

1. **Historical excavation** (not contemporary trends)
2. **Marginalized voices** (African American, Indigenous authors prioritized)
3. **Regional American identity** (not European or fusion)
4. **Preservation techniques** (traditional food technologies)

The 100-600 structure follows Dewey logic:
- 000 = Generalities (reference, theory)
- 100 = Philosophy/History (context, labor)
- 300 = Social Sciences (Indigenous foodways as living culture)
- 400 = Language/Literature (African American voices as recovered texts)
- 500 = Sciences (regional American ecology/place)
- 600 = Technology (preservation as applied technique)

Non-Dewey customization: emphasis on author race/ethnicity and historiographic value reflects
the library's research purpose (excavation, not comprehensiveness).
